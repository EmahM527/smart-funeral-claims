Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Guide

Smart Funeral Claims — Answers to the most common claims questions

About the Claims Process

Q: How do I start a funeral insurance claim?
A: Log in to your account at smartfuneralclaims.com. From your dashboard, click "New Claim", select the claim type, and follow the step-by-step guide. You will need to provide the deceased's details, your details as claimant, and upload the required supporting documents.
Q: How long does it take to process a claim?
A: Most claims are processed within 3–7 business days once all required documents have been received and verified. Premium plan holders receive priority processing with a 24-hour SLA. Complex cases or incomplete submissions may take longer.
Q: How long do I have to submit a claim after the death?
A: You must submit your claim within 6 months of the date of death. Claims submitted after this period may be declined unless exceptional circumstances apply, in which case a formal request for an extension must be submitted in writing.
Q: Can I submit a claim on behalf of someone else?
A: Yes. A nominated beneficiary or an authorised legal representative may submit the claim. You will need to provide proof of your relationship to the deceased and your own identity document, as well as any letter of authority or power of attorney if applicable.
Q: What happens if my claim is rejected?
A: You will receive a written rejection notice with a clear explanation of the reason. You have the right to appeal within 30 days by submitting an appeal letter and any additional supporting documentation to appeals@smartfuneralclaims.com.

Documents & Uploads

Q: What documents are required to file a claim?
A: The mandatory documents are: (1) certified copy of the Death Certificate, (2) certified copy of the deceased's SA ID or passport, (3) certified copy of the claimant's ID, (4) original policy document, and (5) 3-month bank statement. Additional documents such as a marriage certificate or medical records may be required depending on the claim type.
Q: What file formats are accepted?
A: We accept PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX files up to 10 MB per document. Ensure all scans are clear, fully legible, and include all four corners of the document. Blurry, cropped, or incomplete documents will be returned for resubmission.
Q: Do documents need to be certified?
A: Yes. Identity documents, the death certificate, and the policy document must be certified by a Commissioner of Oaths (e.g., a police officer, magistrate, attorney, or bank official). Certifications must not be older than 3 months at the time of submission.

Policy & Cover

Q: What is the waiting period on my policy?
A: For natural death, a 6-month waiting period applies from the policy inception date. There is no waiting period for accidental death. If you added a new beneficiary, the 6-month waiting period applies from the date they were added.
Q: Who can be covered under my policy?
A: Depending on your plan, you can cover yourself (main member), your spouse, up to 4 children (under 21 or full-time students under 26), parents, in-laws, and extended family members. Refer to your policy schedule for the specific members included.
Q: What is not covered by my funeral policy?
A: Exclusions include death by suicide within 24 months of policy inception, death resulting from war or civil unrest, death caused by criminal activity of the claimant, fraudulent claims, and claims submitted more than 6 months after the date of death. Please refer to your policy document for the full list of exclusions.

Security & Privacy

Q: Is my personal information safe on the platform?
A: Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are fully compliant with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and use Firebase's enterprise-grade security infrastructure. Your data will never be sold or shared with third parties without your consent.
Q: Can I track the status of my claim?
A: Yes. Once your claim is submitted, you can log in to your dashboard to track real-time status updates. You will also receive email and SMS notifications at each stage of the review process.
