Exclusions

Claims will not be paid in circumstances including but not limited to:

  • Death resulting from suicide within 24 months of policy inception.
  • Death resulting from war, military operations, or civil unrest.
  • Death caused by the claimant's own criminal activity.
  • Fraudulent claims or misrepresentation of information.
  • Claims submitted outside the 6-month claim period from date of death.

How to Claim

1. Log in to smartfuneralclaims.com or visit our offices.
2. Submit the completed Claim Form with all supporting documents.
3. Our team will assess your claim within 2 business days of receiving all documents.
4. Approved claims are paid directly to the nominated beneficiary's bank account.
