Document Submission Checklist

Smart Funeral Claims — Ensure all required documents are included before submitting your claim.

Use this checklist to verify that all documents are ready before uploading or submitting in person. Incomplete submissions may delay your claim.

REQUIRED — Must be submitted with every claim CONDITIONAL — Required only in specific circumstances

Section A – Identity & Death Documentation

DocumentStatusNotes✓
Death Certificate (official, certified copy) REQUIRED Issued by the Department of Home Affairs. Must be certified within the last 3 months. ☐
Deceased's SA ID / Passport (certified copy) REQUIRED Certified by a Commissioner of Oaths or SAPS officer. ☐
Claimant's SA ID Document (certified copy) REQUIRED The claimant must be the named beneficiary or an authorised representative. ☐
Post-Mortem / Autopsy Report CONDITIONAL Required if the cause of death is relevant to the policy exclusions or is unnatural. ☐

Section B – Policy Documentation

DocumentStatusNotes✓
Insurance Policy Certificate REQUIRED Original or certified copy. Must clearly show policy number and beneficiaries. ☐
Completed & Signed Claim Form REQUIRED Download the official form from the Smart Funeral Claims portal. ☐

Section C – Financial Documentation

DocumentStatusNotes✓
3-Month Bank Statement (claimant) REQUIRED Must be stamped and signed by the bank. Not older than 3 months. Must match the account for payout. ☐
Proof of Banking Details (cancelled cheque / letter) CONDITIONAL Required if the bank statement does not clearly show the account holder name and number. ☐

Section D – Proof of Relationship

DocumentStatusNotes✓
Marriage Certificate CONDITIONAL Required if the claimant is the spouse of the deceased. ☐
Birth Certificate CONDITIONAL Required if claiming for a child or if proving parent-child relationship. ☐
Affidavit of Relationship CONDITIONAL Required for common-law relationships, extended family, or any relationship not evidenced by a certificate. ☐
Letter of Authority / Power of Attorney CONDITIONAL Required if the claimant is acting on behalf of the estate or a minor beneficiary. ☐

Section E – Medical Documentation

DocumentStatusNotes✓
Medical Records / Hospital Report CONDITIONAL Required if the cause of death relates to a pre-existing condition or is subject to policy review. ☐
Attending Physician's Statement CONDITIONAL May be requested by the claims assessor during the review process. ☐
Important: All certified documents must be certified by a Commissioner of Oaths (e.g., police officer, magistrate, bank official, or notary). Certifications must not be older than 3 months at time of submission. Electronic uploads must be clear, legible, and complete — partial or blurry documents will be rejected.
Accepted File Formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX — maximum 10 MB per file. Ensure scanned documents are upright and fully visible including all corners.
