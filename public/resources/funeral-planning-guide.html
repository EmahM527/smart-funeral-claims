Funeral Planning Guide

Smart Funeral Claims — A Practical Guide for Families During a Difficult Time

Losing a loved one is one of life's most challenging experiences. This guide has been prepared to help you navigate the practical steps involved in arranging a funeral and filing an insurance claim, so you can focus on what truly matters — family and healing.

Immediate Steps After a Passing

1

Notify a Medical Professional

A registered doctor or hospital must certify the death. A Death Notice (BI-1663 form) will be completed at this stage.

2

Contact a Funeral Home

The funeral home will help with transportation of the deceased and begin arrangements. They can also assist with obtaining the Death Certificate.

3

Obtain the Death Certificate

Apply at the Department of Home Affairs (DHA). A certified copy of the Death Certificate is required for the insurance claim and estate processes.

4

Notify the Insurance Provider

Contact Smart Funeral Claims as soon as possible. You have up to 6 months from the date of death to submit your claim.

5

Arrange the Funeral Service

Plan the service, catering, transport, and any religious or cultural requirements. Your funeral cover can assist with costs once the claim is approved.

Funeral Arrangement Checklist

  • Obtain the official Death Certificate from the Department of Home Affairs
  • Select a registered funeral home / undertaker
  • Choose between burial or cremation
  • Select a burial site or cremation facility
  • Arrange the funeral service (date, time, venue)
  • Notify family, friends, employer, and relevant institutions
  • Prepare an obituary / death notice for newspapers if desired
  • Arrange catering / refreshments for guests
  • Organise transportation for the family
  • Arrange music, flowers, and order of service
  • Plan tombstone / memorial (if applicable)
  • File the insurance claim with supporting documents
Tip: Keep certified copies of all documents — the Death Certificate, IDs, and policy documents. You may need them for multiple institutions including the bank, Home Affairs, and employer.

Required Documents for the Insurance Claim

  • Certified copy of the Death Certificate
  • Certified copy of the deceased's ID / passport
  • Certified copy of the claimant's SA ID
  • Original insurance policy document
  • 3-month bank statement (claimant)
  • Proof of relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificate, or affidavit)
  • Medical records or post-mortem report (if applicable)
  • Completed and signed Claim Form
