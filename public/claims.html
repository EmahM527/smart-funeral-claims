How to File a Claim

1

Register / Login

Create your account or log in to access the claims portal securely.

2

Start a New Claim

Click "New Claim" on your dashboard and select the claim type (death, disability, etc.).

3

Fill in Details

Provide the policy number, claimant details, and information about the deceased.

4

Upload Documents

Securely upload all required supporting documents directly through the portal.

5

Submit & Track

Submit your claim and track its progress in real time with instant notifications.

6

Receive Payout

Once approved, funds are transferred directly to the nominated beneficiary account.

Required Documents

Death Certificate

Official certified copy issued by the Department of Home Affairs.

ID of Deceased

Certified copy of the deceased's South African ID or passport.

Claimant's ID

Certified copy of the claimant's valid SA ID document.

Policy Document

Copy of the funeral insurance policy certificate.

Bank Statement

Most recent 3-month bank statement for payout verification.

Proof of Relationship

Marriage certificate, birth certificate, or affidavit where applicable.

Medical Records

Required if cause of death is relevant to the policy claim type.

Completed Claim Form

Fully completed and signed claim form (available in Resources).
