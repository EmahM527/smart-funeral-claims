How to File a Claim

Follow these 7 steps to submit your claim quickly and correctly.

1

Register / Login

Create your free account or log in to access the secure claims portal.

2

Start a New Claim

Click "New Claim" on your dashboard and select the appropriate claim type.

3

Fill in Claim Details

Enter the policy number, claimant information, and details about the deceased.

4

Upload Documents

Securely upload certified copies of all required supporting documents (PDF / JPG / PNG).

5

Submit Claim

Review all your information and submit. You'll receive an instant email confirmation.

6

Track Your Claim

Monitor real-time progress on your dashboard. Receive SMS and email notifications at every stage.

7

Receive Payout

Once approved, funds are transferred directly to the nominated beneficiary's bank account.

Tips for a Smooth Claim

Avoid common delays with these simple steps.

Certify all documents

Every copy must be certified by a Commissioner of Oaths. Certifications must be dated within the last 3 months.

Scan clearly

Use a flat scan or a well-lit photo with all four corners visible. Blurry or cropped documents will be returned.

Submit within 6 months

Claims must be submitted within 6 months of the date of death. Don't wait — early submission speeds up payout.

Keep your email active

All status updates and requests for additional info are sent via email. Check your inbox and spam folder regularly.

Verify bank details

Ensure the account holder name matches the claimant's ID exactly. Mismatched details delay or block payouts.
